Checkmait Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026 · Last updated: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Checkmait ("Checkmait," "we," "us," or "our") collects when you use the Checkmait mobile application (the "App"), how we use that information, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Checkmait is an AI-assisted training tool for student and certificated pilots that simulates a Designated Pilot Examiner ("DPE") oral exam and Air Traffic Control ("ATC") radio communications.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information

When you sign up or sign in, our authentication provider (Clerk) collects and stores your email address and, depending on the sign-in method you choose, your name and a profile image. Clerk also assigns you a unique user identifier.

1.2 Voice and audio recordings

When you start a practice session and speak into your device's microphone, the App captures your spoken audio for the duration of that session. The audio is captured solely so it can be transcribed and used to generate the AI examiner's or AI controller's response.

1.3 Session content

The choices you make to start a practice session (e.g., the checkride task, ATC scenario, aircraft type, airport identifier, weather conditions) are sent to our backend so it can construct the correct AI prompt. Saved session transcripts are stored on your device by default.

1.4 Diagnostic and crash information

If the App crashes or encounters a runtime error, our crash-reporting provider (Sentry) collects a crash report that includes device model, operating-system version, App version, and a stack trace. The crash report is associated with your Clerk user identifier so we can debug issues affecting specific accounts.

1.5 Subscription and billing information

Checkmait does not currently offer paid subscriptions in the App. When subscriptions are introduced in a future version, purchases made on iOS will use Apple's In-App Purchase system, and Apple will be the merchant of record. If subscriptions are also offered through our website, our billing provider (Stripe) will collect and process payment information for those purchases. Checkmait does not store full payment-card data on its own servers.

1.6 Information we do not collect

  • Your phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, or calendar.
  • Your real-time location.
  • Health or biometric data.
  • Advertising or marketing identifiers.
  • Data collected for tracking across other apps or websites.

2. How We Collect Information

Account information is collected by Clerk during sign-up and sign-in flows shown inside the App. Audio is collected only while you are inside an active practice session and only after you have granted iOS microphone permission and accepted the in-App AI disclosure described in Section 5; the microphone is not active in the background or outside an active session. Session content is collected from your selections on practice-setup screens. Crash and diagnostic information is collected automatically by Sentry when an error occurs.

3. How We Use the Information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To transcribe your spoken audio and generate the AI examiner's or AI controller's responses in real time.
  • To display session transcripts and progress to you on your device.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes, bugs, and performance issues.
  • To provide customer support if you contact us.
  • If you purchase a subscription, to provision and manage your entitlement and to comply with tax and accounting obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties. We do not build behavioral profiles. We do not analyze your audio for any purpose other than producing the AI training response that you requested.

5. AI Disclosure and Consent

Before any audio leaves your device, the App displays an AI-disclosure-and-consent screen that names OpenAI as the AI provider, explains that microphone audio recorded during practice sessions and the session-context prompt will be sent to OpenAI for processing, states that this data is not used to train OpenAI's models, links to this Privacy Policy, and requires your explicit "I Agree and Continue" tap before proceeding.

You may revoke your consent at any time inside the App at Settings → Privacy → AI Services. Revoking consent disables AI-dependent features but leaves the rest of the App usable. Independently, iOS will request microphone permission the first time the App tries to access your microphone, and you can revoke that permission at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Data Retention

  • Account information: retained by Clerk for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the associated record is deleted by Clerk and removed from our database.
  • Audio: not stored on our servers. Audio is streamed to OpenAI for processing and is retained by OpenAI only for the limited abuse-monitoring period described in their API data-usage policies, after which they delete it.
  • Session transcripts: stored locally on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Crash reports: retained by Sentry for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Subscription records (when applicable): retained for as long as the subscription is active and for the period required by tax and accounting law thereafter.

7. Security

All data sent from the App is transmitted over HTTPS using industry-standard transport encryption (TLS 1.2 or later). Account credentials are managed by Clerk under their security program. Our backend is hosted on Railway and access is restricted to authorized personnel. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect your information.

8. Children's Privacy

Checkmait is intended for users who are at least 13 years old and pursuing or holding a pilot certificate. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and provided information, please contact us and we will delete the information promptly.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to data portability. You can exercise these rights at any time by:

  • Editing your profile in the App.
  • Revoking AI-services consent in Settings → Privacy → AI Services.
  • Revoking iOS microphone permission in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting saved transcripts from within the App.
  • Deleting your account from Settings → Account → Delete Account, which removes your record from Clerk and our backend.
  • Uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data from the device.
  • Contacting us with any privacy request, including a request that we ask OpenAI, Sentry, Clerk, or Stripe to delete data they hold about you.

10. International Users

Checkmait is operated from the United States. If you use the App from another country, the information you provide will be transmitted to and processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction. By using the App and granting consent on the disclosure screen, you consent to this transfer.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, we will display an updated consent screen inside the App so you can review and accept the changes before continuing to use the AI features.

12. Contact Us
